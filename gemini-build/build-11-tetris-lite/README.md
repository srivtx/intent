# Build 11: Tetris-Lite (`tetris.intent` & `gen_tetris.py`)

## 1. What We Built
A falling-block **Tetris-lite** game ([`tetris.intent`](file:///Users/zen/Desktop/project-10nov/gemini-build/build-11-tetris-lite/tetris.intent), generated by [`gen_tetris.py`](file:///Users/zen/Desktop/project-10nov/gemini-build/build-11-tetris-lite/gen_tetris.py)) on an `8 x 14` board where settled blocks accumulate in a `Cells` collection, full horizontal rows clear and shift all rows above them down by 1, and gravity accelerates as more lines are cleared (`The delay is always 2 if the lines are at least 18, otherwise 8 minus the lines divided by 3.`).

---

## 2. Why a Generator Was Needed & 4 Compiler Findings (Notes for the Language Maintainer)

### Finding 1: No Per-Key Group Counts or Dynamic Timer Periods
- **Why `gen_tetris.py` exists**: To detect when row `k` is full (has 8 settled cells), Intent v0.6.0 has no `count of cells whose y is k` expression inside event conditions and no 1D arrays. Therefore, tracking row occupancy requires one scalar variable per row (`H = 14` variables) and `O(H^2)` sentences (`206` sentences total) to shift upper row counters down whenever row `k` clears.
- **Variable-speed gravity**: Timer periods (`Every 50 milliseconds`) must be literal constants. To make blocks fall faster as `lines` increases, we used a software clock (`the clock increases by 1` every 50ms) compared against a reactive standing rule (`The delay is always 2 if the lines are at least 18, otherwise 8 minus the lines divided by 3.`).

### Finding 2: Spellchecker False Positive on Numbered Declarations (`fill1`, `fill2`, ...)
- **What we tried first**: Declaring per-row counters `The fill1 starts at 0.` followed by `The fill2 starts at 0.`.
- **What happened**: Rejected by [`intentc/parser.py:1115-1118`](file:///Users/zen/Desktop/project-10nov/intentc/parser.py#L1115-L1118):
  ```text
  error: I don't know the word "fill2" — did you mean "fill1"?
  ```
  Because `decl_value` checks whether any new identifier of length > 3 has edit distance `<= 1` from an existing identifier (intended to catch `"The scroe is 5."`), any numbered series of declarations (`fill1`, `fill2`, `row1`, `row2`) is rejected as a typo of the first!
- **Workaround**: Named the 14 row counters after distinct NATO phonetic words (`alpha`, `bravo`, `charlie`, ..., `november`).
- **Hypothesis for Maintainer**: In `decl_value` (`parser.py:1117`), exempt words that end in digits (`w[-1].isdigit()`) or sentences that explicitly use `starts at` / `is a number` from the typo-distance check against other declared values.

### Finding 3: `any` / `no` Quantified Clauses Must Appear First in Compound Conditions
- **What we tried first**:
  ```intent
  When the left key is pressed, if the px is over 1 and no cell has an x equal to the px minus 1 and a y equal to the py, the px decreases by 1.
  ```
- **What happened**: Rejected with `error: I don't know the word "no"`. `split_other` / `quantified` only recognizes `no <coll>` when it immediately follows `if`/`when`, not after `and`.
- **Workaround**: Put `if no cell has ...` at the front and `if the px is over 1` at the end:
  ```intent
  When the left key is pressed, if no cell has an x equal to the px minus 1 and a y equal to the py, the px decreases by 1 if the px is over 1.
  ```

### Finding 4: `increase ... whose ... by 1 if ...` Swallows Trailing `if` into `main`
- **What we tried first**:
  ```intent
  Every 50 milliseconds, increase the y of the cells whose y is under 1 by 1 if the clear is 1.
  ```
- **What happened**: Rejected with `error: I don't see how "if clear is 1" fits in`. In [`intentc/parser.py:1828-1832`](file:///Users/zen/Desktop/project-10nov/intentc/parser.py#L1828-L1832), when `by <amount>` appears after `whose`, the parser slices `cond_t[b:]` (everything from `by` to the end of the sentence) back into `main`, inadvertently pulling `if the clear is 1` into `parse_updates`!
- **Workaround**: Place `by 1` **before** the `if` condition:
  ```intent
  Every 50 milliseconds, increase each cell's y by 1 if its y is under 1 and the clear is 1.
  ```
